MaaBookingCase Study

MaaBooking is a platform that enables users to discover and book farmhouses, events, and community experiences. The goal was to simplify the booking process and improve user confidence.

Role UI/UX Designer
Duration 3 Weeks (Continuously improving)
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Platform Web & Mobile App
Weekend escape

MaaBooking

Overview

Designing trust into a high-intent booking flow.

Problem Statement

Users struggle to find reliable platforms for booking farmhouses with clear pricing, availability, and multiple experience options.

Design Goal

Simplify the discovery-to-booking journey while making categories, price clarity, and trust signals easy to scan.

Research

User insights shaped the product priorities.

01

Quick Booking

Users wanted a shorter path from search to confirmation.

02

Clear Visuals

Property images needed to feel useful, current, and easy to compare.

03

Transparent Pricing

Users needed visible costs, availability, and options before committing.

Persona

Rahul, 26

Rahul wants to book a farmhouse for weekend plans without jumping between confusing platforms or calling multiple owners.

Goal Book a farmhouse quickly for weekends. Frustrations Unclear pricing, weak trust signals, and confusing filters.
Journey

From search to confirmation.

  1. Search
  2. Browse
  3. View
  4. Book
  5. Confirm
Solution

A cleaner interface for confident decisions.

Multi-category booking

Farmhouses, events, and experiences are separated into clear entry points.

Filters and search

Users can narrow listings by location, price, availability, and activity type.

Fast booking flow

The final flow reduces uncertainty with focused details and confirmation cues.

Outcome

A scalable booking experience that improves usability and trust.

Learnings

Simplicity matters

The strongest screens were the ones that reduced choices at the right moment.

Hierarchy

Structure builds confidence

Clear visual order helped users understand listings faster.

Future

Reviews and payments

Next improvements include user reviews, payment integration, and personalization.
